## 👋 About me

Senior SAP Consulting / Software & Data Architecture in Brazil, with projects across Latin America, **20 years in IT** and SAP from both sides of the table: I implemented **SAP Business One** as IT manager at an industrial company, and today I design add-ons, integrations and data structures at an **SAP Gold Partner** software house focused on agribusiness.

- 🧭 I translate business rules and user problems into **specifications and data models**, and guide AI to turn them into reviewed code.
- 🗄️ Databases I administer and model: **SAP HANA, SQL Server, Oracle, PostgreSQL and Firebird**.
- 🤖 I run **spec-driven development (SDD) with Claude** (Claude Code, skills and MCP), from the client ticket to the approved pull request.
- 🌾 Strong domain knowledge in **agribusiness** (cotton, seeds, grains, fertilizers), **finance, credit, imports and banking integration**.
- 🔭 Currently: **clean core** architecture for SAP B1, integrations via **Service Layer / SAP BTP** and AI applied to ERP.
